VENDOR SERVICE AGREEMENT
Acme Analytics Platform — Standard Terms

1. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated
as provided herein. Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon 45 days' prior written notice to the other party. Either
party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the
other party commits a material breach that remains uncured 15 days after
receiving written notice describing the breach.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

Subscription fees are invoiced monthly in advance and are due within 30
days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month
or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3. REFUNDS

Refunds for prepaid, unused subscription fees will be issued within 10
business days of the effective termination date, prorated to the nearest
full month remaining on the prepaid term. Fees for services already
rendered, including partial months of usage, are non-refundable.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party agrees to protect the other party's confidential information
using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own
confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care.
Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a
period of 3 years.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Except for breaches of the confidentiality section, neither party's total
liability arising out of this Agreement will exceed the fees paid by
Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

6. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without
regard to its conflict of laws principles.
