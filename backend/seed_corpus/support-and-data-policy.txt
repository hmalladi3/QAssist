SUPPORT AND DATA HANDLING POLICY
Acme Analytics Platform

1. SUPPORT HOURS AND RESPONSE TIMES

Standard support requests submitted through the help portal receive an
initial response within 4 business hours during weekday business hours
(9am-6pm ET, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays). Critical
production-down issues reported through the emergency escalation line are
addressed within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

2. DATA RETENTION

Customer data uploaded to the platform is retained for 90 days following
the customer's last active session. After that 90-day window, data is
automatically deleted from all production systems. Backups containing
that data are rotated out and permanently purged within an additional 30
days, meaning no copy of the data exists anywhere after 120 days of
inactivity.

3. DATA EXPORT

Customers may request a full export of their data at any time prior to
deletion by submitting a request through the help portal. Exports are
delivered as a downloadable archive within 5 business days of the
request.

4. SECURITY INCIDENT NOTIFICATION

In the event of a confirmed security incident affecting customer data,
affected customers will be notified by email within 72 hours of
confirmation, including a description of the incident, the data
involved, and remediation steps taken.

5. ACCEPTABLE USE

Customers may not use the platform to store or process data that violates
applicable law, infringes third-party intellectual property rights, or
contains malicious code.
